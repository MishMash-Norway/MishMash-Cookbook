# Taking part

MishMash is a network more than a building, and most of what it does depends on people joining in. This chapter explains the ways in: as a member, a partner, a Master Fellow, a seed project or a visitor to the events. The website's [FAQ](https://mishmash.no/faq/) answers the practical questions in more detail.

## Joining the network

Anyone whose work concerns AI and creativity can join: researchers, artists, educators, students, and people in the cultural sector, the public sector and business. The centre's main task is to connect researchers and institutions in Norway, but members based abroad, and members without a current institutional affiliation, are also welcome.

Members register through a [participation form](https://nettskjema.no/a/625226) on Nettskjema. The form asks for affiliation, identifiers and links, the work packages the person wants to join, and the level of involvement. A full member does most of their research within the centre's themes and takes part in all activities; an associate member takes part from time to time; an affiliate member follows what is going on. Every member needs an ORCID identifier, and registering again with the same form updates the details.

Each member chooses one home work package and may join others too; the weekly MeshUps are a good way to find the right home. Membership brings no funding and costs nothing, but members can apply for seed funding with other partners.

## The people directory

Members who agree to be listed appear in the [people directory](https://mishmash.no/search/?type=person) on the website, with name, role, affiliation, identifiers and chosen links; a portrait needs a separate consent. Results and affiliations are refreshed every night from NVA and ORCID. The [privacy notice](https://mishmash.no/privacy/) explains what is shown and how to have it changed or removed.

## Partners

Institutions, companies and organisations can become associate partners by writing to the centre. Partnership gives access to the network, the mailing lists and the events, and brings no funding and no fee. Partners are expected to take part in work package activities, share relevant events and help develop joint applications. For businesses and public organisations, the usual route in is a concrete collaboration with a work package, such as a joint project, a workshop or an industry doctorate.

## MeshUps

The MeshUp is the centre's weekly meeting point: 30 minutes on Zoom, on Thursdays at 12:00 during the semesters. After a few minutes of news from the management, one speaker gives a 15-minute presentation, followed by questions. The link goes to the members of the work packages, and others can ask for it. MeshUps are not recorded. They are listed on the [events page](https://mishmash.no/events/), with a calendar to subscribe to.

## Master Fellowships

Since August 2026, master's students at partner and affiliated institutions can become MishMash Master Fellows. The fellowship is unpaid. Fellows take full part in the activities of one or more work packages, can get funding to attend centre events such as the conferences, can apply for seed funding, and are listed in the directory. The main requirement is a thesis or project that touches on AI and creativity, with a supervisor who is active in the centre. Applications go through a [short online form](https://nettskjema.no/a/mishmash-master-fellows), and fellows are selected by the work package leaders together with the management group.

## Seed funding

Seed funding supports small joint projects that start collaborations between partners. A project can receive up to NOK 50,000, for seminars and workshops, master's scholarships and research assistants, moderate travel, production costs, and fees for contributors from outside the partner institutions. Licences, tokens and equipment are covered only when clearly justified, and licences preferably as network or site licences that many partners can use. The centre does not buy subscriptions to commercial AI services for its members.

Every project must end in something that can be published, such as a report, an article, code, a dataset, an artwork or teaching material. Applications are assessed by the management and discussed with the work package leaders, with weight on relevance, collaboration across partners and sectors, and outputs that others can use.

The first round had its deadline on 2 March 2026 and the second on 1 June 2026; the third closes on 15 January 2027. The funded projects are listed among the [projects](https://mishmash.no/projects/) on the website, and the calls are announced on the work package mailing lists. Applicants should first talk to the most relevant work package leader.

## Vacancies

Doctoral and postdoctoral fellowships are announced by the partner institutions and collected on the [open positions](https://mishmash.no/vacancies/) page, which also covers the Master Fellowships. Internships are usually not announced, but agreed case by case with a work package leader or another member of the consortium.

## Conferences and events

The centre holds a conference each semester, hosted in turn by the partners. After the opening conference in Kristiansand in September 2026, the second is [AR@K27](https://mishmash.no/events/kristiania2027/) at Kristiania University of Applied Sciences on 13 and 14 April 2027, followed by the University of Inland Norway in the autumn of 2027. The conferences and the MeshUps are open to people outside the centre; work package events are usually for members. Anyone can subscribe to the [announcements list](https://sympa.uio.no/mishmash.no/subscribe/announcements).

## Getting in touch

General questions go to [contact@mishmash.no](mailto:contact@mishmash.no). Questions about a work package go to its leaders, who are listed on each work package page and in the [Work Package Leader Group](https://mishmash.no/about/organisation/wp-leaders/). News, events and results can be sent to the same address or registered in NVA, from where the website picks them up every night.
