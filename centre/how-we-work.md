# How we work: the cookbook

This chapter is the cookbook proper: the recurring recipes that make a centre of more than 200 people across more than 20 institutions work as one environment. Each recipe says what it is, who runs it and where to find it. The chapter grows as the centre finds out what works.

## MeshUp

A weekly online seminar of 30 minutes on Zoom, on Thursdays at noon. One presenter from one work package, a short talk and questions. The first MeshUp was held on 29 January 2026, and the series is listed under [events](https://mishmash.no/events/) on the website. The MeshUp is the one meeting everyone in the centre can attend, and the easiest way to learn what the other work packages are doing.

## Work package meetings

Each work package meets online every second week, and in person at the centre's conferences. The work package leader group meets separately to coordinate across packages. The first in-person meetings of most work packages took place at the formal opening on 8 April 2026.

## Webinars and workshops

Work packages run their own webinars, open to the whole centre, and thematic workshops such as the RAVE hackathon at the opening in April 2026 and the workshop on environmentally sustainable use of AI in June 2026.

## Seed funding

Small grants for joint work between partners, intended to start collaborations that can grow into projects, publications or works. The first seed projects reported in 2026. Details are on the website under [internal funding](https://mishmash.no/internal/funding/).

## Fellowships

Doctoral and postdoctoral fellowships are announced at the partner institutions and listed on the [open positions](https://mishmash.no/vacancies/) page. From August 2026 the centre also has unpaid Master Fellowships, which let master's students at partner institutions join a work package, apply for seed funding and get support to attend centre events.

## Conferences

One large gathering each semester. The opening conference took place in Kristiansand on 14 and 15 September 2026, hosted by the University of Agder. The second is AR@K27, the ninth Annual Artistic Research Conference, at Kristiania University of Applied Sciences in Oslo on 13 and 14 April 2027, on the theme AI as Environment.

## The website

The website at mishmash.no is built in the open on GitHub and is itself one of the centre's experiments. It pulls results from NVA and ORCID every night, offers most pages at three reading levels, and declares in an [AI colophon](https://mishmash.no/about/ai-colophon/) how AI is used in making it. Anyone in the centre can propose a change through the repository.

## Open results

Publications, artistic results, datasets and software are registered in NVA, the national research information system, under the centre's project record, and appear on the website's [results](https://mishmash.no/results/) page without manual editing. Content on the website and in this book is released under a Creative Commons Attribution licence unless stated otherwise.
