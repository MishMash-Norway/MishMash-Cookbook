# 2027: year 2

**Status.** Not yet written. The chapter follows the same structure as the earlier years: the year in brief, research, people, events and numbers.

The third conference is planned at the University of Inland Norway in the autumn. The Board meets on 15 February and 19 April, the second time together with the work package leaders, and the Stakeholder Board is planned for the spring.
