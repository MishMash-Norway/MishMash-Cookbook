# 2025: year 0, on paper

**Status.** The year the centre was funded and started on paper, on 1 December 2025. Eleven months of application and preparation, one month of formal existence.

## The year in brief

- 15 January: the application is submitted, one of 50.
- 4 April: 19 applications go to the final round; MishMash is interviewed on 23 April.
- 11 June: the Research Council announces six national AI centres. MishMash Centre for AI and Creativity is one of them.
- 20 June: grant letter, up to 173 million kroner over five years, subject to a revised application submitted in September.
- September and October: the work package leader group meets in workshops and sets up the centre's structures.
- November: the first doctoral and postdoctoral fellowships are prepared for announcement.
- 1 December: the centre formally starts. The contract with the Research Council is signed in December.
- 3 December: the first public event, a concert and presentation of generative music with AI and quantum computing, at the University of Oslo.

## People

The management group and the work package leader group were in place by the autumn. Their members are listed under [organisation](../centre/organisation.md) and on the website.
