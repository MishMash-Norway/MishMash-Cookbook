# 2024: year −1, planning

**Status.** The year before the centre existed on paper. The idea took shape, the sketch and the consortium were built, and the series starts here because the work did.

## The year in brief

- February: the Research Council announces the national AI centres.
- April and May: the idea is presented to the national body for music, and a national online workshop on music and AI is held on 2 May.
- June: the sketch is submitted by the deadline of 7 June, one of 122.
- September: feedback on the sketch, and the National Workshop on AI and Creativity on 25 September, where the consortium takes its shape.
- October to December: administrative workshop, the first council meeting on 15 November, and letters of intent from partners.

## People

A small group at the University of Oslo, with the RITMO Centre of Excellence and the CreaTeME Centre of Excellence in Education as the two starting points, and colleagues at partner institutions who joined through the two workshops.
