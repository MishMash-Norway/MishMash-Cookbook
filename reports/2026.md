# 2026: year 1, the launch

**Status.** In progress. This chapter is written during the year and completed after it; everything below is as of November 2026.

## A word from the management

MishMash started on paper on 1 December 2025, but 2026 is the year it came to life. In January the administrative coordinator started and the first MeshUp gathered the network online on a Thursday at noon; by November the MeshUps had become the one meeting everyone in the centre can join. In April the centre was opened in the University Aula, with the rector and the Minister of Research and Higher Education, and in September researchers, artists, students and people from the cultural sector met in Kristiansand for the first conference. In between, the fellows arrived, the work packages found their rhythm, and the Board and the Council met for the first times.

It has also been a year of finding out how a centre spread over the whole country works. RITMO was a centre where people met at the coffee machine; MishMash is more like a festival tent, put up wherever the network gathers. The facts and figures at the end of this chapter show how much has happened already; the work packages tell their own stories below.

## The year at a glance

- 21 January: the administrative coordinator starts.
- 29 January: the first MeshUp, the centre's weekly online seminar.
- March: the consortium agreement is signed by all partners; the Council is appointed on 16 March and the Board on 27 March.
- 8 April: formal opening at the University of Oslo. A research application workshop with the Research Council, a RAVE hackathon on biosignals and real-time audio, work package meetings, a panel on AI in creative practice, and the opening ceremony in the University Aula with the rector and the Minister of Research and Higher Education, followed by performances from across the centre.
- 10 April: first Board meeting; the Board met again on 26 June, 14 September and 3 November.
- 30 April: first Council meeting; the second was on 22 October.
- Spring: doctoral and postdoctoral fellowships announced at partner institutions.
- June: workshop on environmentally sustainable use of AI, a seed-funded project between NORSUS and SINTEF Digital.
- 17 August: the Master Fellowship programme opens.
- 14 and 15 September: the opening conference in Kristiansand, hosted by the University of Agder in cooperation with Kilden Performing Arts Centre, Kunstsilo, Kraftverk and Cultiva. Keynotes by Jane C. Ginsburg, Gerfried Stocker, Sarah Ellis and Jon McCormack; three parallel tracks on humans, machines and society; seven thematic sessions; about forty posters; a mini-festival at Kraftverk with the sound installation The Logos and a presentation of the film Julius; and the installation-performance .skin at Kunstsilo. The second day was held back to back with Tech:Art for the cultural field.
- 4 November: roundtable in the government quarter with the Minister of Research and Higher Education and the Minister of Digitalisation and Public Governance for the national AI centres. MishMash was one of five centres asked to open, on AI and copyright.

## Highlights

### The opening, 8 April

The centre was opened at the University of Oslo with a full day: a research application workshop with the Research Council, a RAVE hackathon on biosignals and real-time audio, work package meetings, a panel on AI in creative practice, and in the evening the opening ceremony in the University Aula, followed by performances from across the centre.

### The opening conference, 14 and 15 September

Kristiansand hosted the first conference, at Kilden Performing Arts Centre, with Kunstsilo, Kraftverk and Cultiva as partners. There were keynotes by Jane C. Ginsburg, Gerfried Stocker, Sarah Ellis and Jon McCormack, three parallel tracks on humans, machines and society, seven thematic sessions and about forty posters. In the evening MishMash LIVE filled Kraftverk with music, and the second day was held back to back with Tech:Art for the cultural field.

### Meeting the ministers, 4 November

The Minister of Research and Higher Education and the Minister of Digitalisation and Public Governance invited the national AI centres to a roundtable in the government quarter. MishMash was one of five centres asked to open, and spoke about AI and copyright: what a Norwegian multimodal model trained on the National Library's collection could do, if it is built with traceability from the start. The written follow-up is the centre's input to the new national AI strategy, due 20 November.

## People

Recruitment of the first cohort of fellows ran through the spring and summer; by September more than twenty people had been hired and the rest were in process (Board, 14 September). In August the Master Fellowship programme opened, so that master's students at the partner institutions can join a work package. The people directory on the website lists everyone in the network, and [organisation](../centre/organisation.md) describes who does what.
